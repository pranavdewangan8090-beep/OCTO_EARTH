Quick start

    
    
      
  1. Watch a mission: press ▶ in the bottom bar (or Space). The hand-designed heuristic works without training.
    2. 
      
  2. Speed: drag the slider. Slow = single packets and the Moon; fast = the whole spiral.
    3. 
      
  3. Earth POV: press V or the ◉ View button for the close-up with selectable layers (magnetic field, plasma, gravity, Mars).
    4. 
      
  4. Power beams (button in the bottom bar or key B): ride on an arm and watch the microwave beams from the solar collectors arrive and be absorbed whenever a packet is launched.
    5. 
      
  5. Weather (key W): Earth with clouds, winds, ice and its living zones, kept warm far from the Sun by a ring of space mirrors – switch the mirrors off to see the frozen Earth.
    6. 
      
  6. Collector (key C): a close-up of one of the 5 million solar collector satellites – solar wings facing the Sun, microwave transmitter beaming to Earth's arms.
    7. 
      
  7. Mars (red) is fully simulated: it pulls Earth and is pulled back. Earth must stay outside the safe distance (red ring) – the safety shield steers around it.
