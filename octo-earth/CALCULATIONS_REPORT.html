1. What the system is and the three enablers

OCTO-EARTH moves Earth (with its Moon) from its orbit at 1 AU to a circular orbit at 2.7 AU within 10,000 simulated years by throwing mass away from Earth (momentum exchange: Earth recoils the other way). The thrown mass comes from an asteroid docked to Earth, the energy comes from sunlight collected near the Sun and beamed to Earth as microwaves, and Earth's climate is kept livable far from the Sun by mirrors that reflect extra sunlight onto it. A reinforcement-learning agent (PPO) chooses every 100 years how hard, how fast and in which direction to throw, and how much mirror light to send to Earth.

Exactly three enablers. An enabler is a technology the model ASSUMES can be built without showing it. The model allows only three; each one is a complete system made of the instruments listed under it. Everything else in the model is ordinary physics (gravity, momentum, energy, radiation) or ordinary control software.

Mercury, Venus and Jupiter (update p35; Saturn and beyond not modelled)

Mercury, Venus and Jupiter start on their real (J2000) orbits and are integrated in both models: they pull on Earth, the Moon (high-fidelity model), Mars and the fragments, and Earth pulls back (two-way gravity).
